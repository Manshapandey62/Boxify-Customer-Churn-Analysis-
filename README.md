# Boxify-Customer-Churn-Analysis-

## 📊 Project Overview
This project analyzes customer churn for Boxify, an online subscription box service. The objective is to identify high-risk customers, understand churn patterns, and provide insights that can support customer retention strategies.

## 🎯 Business Objective
- Identify customers at high risk of churn
- Analyze customer engagement and spending behavior
- Understand churn patterns across customer segments
- Identify potential revenue loss due to churn
- Support data-driven customer retention decisions

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Data Visualization

## 📌 Key KPIs
- Churn Rate
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Engagement Rate
- Subscription Duration
- Retention by Segment
- Revenue Lost Due to Churn

## 📈 Analysis
The dashboard analyzes customer behavior using interactive visuals and risk-level segmentation to identify customers who may require retention attention.

## 💡 Key Insights
- High-risk customers were identified using customer behavior and engagement indicators.
- Customer engagement and spending were analyzed to understand churn risk.
- Customer segments were compared to identify retention patterns.
- Revenue impact associated with churn was analyzed.

## 📊 Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive view of customer churn, customer risk levels, engagement, and spending patterns.
